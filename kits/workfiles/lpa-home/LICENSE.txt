	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Low_Poly_Household_Items/`, one zip of
	.fbx files in category folders under `Low Poly Furniture`. It holds no
	license file and names no author or pack page. Its layout and flat
	materials match the other `lpa` packs, so it carries the same artist
	tag. The slug is lpa-home because `household` is the Creative Trio kit.

	89 of the 112 models are loaded.


	Scale

	0.0009, the factor that leaves the fewest size findings over the kit:
	the fridge lands at 0.85, the chair at 0.44, the single bed at 1.0.
	Small props (mug, plate, vase, bowl) are modelled near real size against
	the furniture, so they sit below their kinds' ranges.


	Colour

	Flat materials are mapped to bands of kits/colormap.png by
	tools/import/adopt.mjs: mid grey to nickel, dark grey to basalt, white
	to ivory, the two woods to chestnut and camel, red to sienna, green to
	hunter, screens and glass to azure. Blue cloth has no textile band and
	takes hunter; blue books slate, blue vase azure as glass, the plant pot
	terracotta, yellow mug terracotta, yellow lamp shade ivory.
