How a curve is fitted

Per kit and kind, the median model size divided by the kind's assumed real size. A Theil–Sen line through those points, in log2 on both axes, . Scatter is the median absolute residual, under the same weighting. A kit needs 3 kinds spanning at least a factor 2 in real size, or it is dropped.

Range is the lowest and highest slope when the line is refitted with each kind left out in turn. A kit is unstable, and drawn dotted, when that range is wider than 0.3, or when no kind can be left out without the kit falling below the minimum.

Buildings

A building's assumed real size is its own height, read from its storeys-N tag: storeys × 3 m + 1.5 m of roof. Buildings carrying no such tag fall back to the flat height in the table below.
