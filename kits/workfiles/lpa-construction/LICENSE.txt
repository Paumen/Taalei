	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Low_Poly_Construction/`, one zip of .fbx
	files with flat materials. It holds no license file and names no
	author or pack page; it is a LowPolyAssets pack, so it carries the
	`lpa` artist tag.

	72 of the 81 models are loaded.


	Scale

	0.0032, the factor that leaves the fewest size findings over the kit:
	the traffic cone lands at 0.47, the portable toilet at 0.93, the
	stepladder at 0.96. The tower crane and the bucket sit outside their
	kinds' ranges at this factor.


	Colour

	Flat source colours are mapped to bands of kits/colormap.png by
	tools/import/adopt.mjs: steel to nickel, concrete and brick to taupe,
	timber to tan, camel and chestnut, black plastic and rubber to basalt,
	purple plastic to azure, red to sienna, orange to terracotta, yellow
	to amber, white to ivory.
