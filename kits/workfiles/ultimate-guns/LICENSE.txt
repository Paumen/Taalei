	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Ultimate_Guns_Pack/Ultimate_Guns_Pack-glb.zip`.
	It holds no license file and names no author or pack page. The CC0 note
	above was set by the owner of this project on import; it does not come from
	the pack itself. The author is unknown, so there is nobody to credit —
	whoever traces the origin adds it here.

	The pack is one .glb per model. 7 of the 25 are loaded: the revolvers and
	shotguns, which are the ones with a design older than the §1 I04 line of
	1850. The assault rifles, bullpups, submachine guns, snipers and mounts are
	left in the missing tab.


	Scale

	One scale factor for the whole pack (style guide §6, P10): 0,20, baked into
	the geometry. It is set by comparison with the catalogue's other firearms:
	`shotgun` runs 1,15 long beside quat-pirate/rifle at 1,10, and `revolver`
	0,40 beside quat-pirate/pistol at 0,50.

	`shotgun` and `shotgun-pump` are 1,15 against the 1,00 limit on
	obj-weapon-ranged — a warning, not an error. The one pack factor wins
	(§6, P10).


	Names

	The pack names in mixed case and hangs a download hash on repeats; here that
	is kebab-case with a name that says what the gun is. The source name is in
	every .glb under `asset.extras.taaleiland.bronmodel`.

  Revolver_1 / _3 / _5       -> revolver, revolver-ribbed, revolver-steel
  Shotgun_2 / _3             -> shotgun, shotgun-pump
  Shotgun SawedOff           -> shotgun-sawed-off
  Shotgun ShortStock         -> shotgun-pistol-grip


	Colour

	The source paints one flat colour per material, seven in all. None of them
	came along: each is mapped to a band of the shared kits/colormap.png and
	placed in that band at the height whose luminance matches the source
	colour, shaded along normal Y, so the relative light-to-dark reading is
	kept.

  Metal, LightMetal, DarkMetal, Grey, Black -> metal-iron-steel, nickel
  Wood                                      -> wood-worked, camel
  DarkWood                                  -> wood-beam, chestnut

	The five source greys differ only in luminance, so they share the nickel
	band and are told apart by their height in it.

	The bands follow §4 and §5 of the style guide.


	What happened to the files is in tools/importeer/flat-packs.mjs: scaled,
	origin at y=0 with the footprint centred, vertices re-welded, normals
	smoothed at 60°, and written as one draw call.

	`shotgun-pistol-grip` keeps one inward-facing face from the source, on the
	ejection port cover.
