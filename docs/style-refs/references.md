## What the PO's notes point to

- Recolour only, with common sense, often lifts a model the most.
- Bold tri cuts that keep the model as recognisable as before: decide which details matter (stool, wooden shield, octopus nigiri, celestial globe).
- Spot glitches and small overlooked fixes (hair dryer slivers, scaffold posts, battle-axe misformed parts).

## Six principles, read blind from the 31 good pairs

An agent saw only the renders, no names or notes.

1. Make the object easy to recognise at a glance.
2. Colours follow the real material; odd colours go.
3. Fewer colours and more unity on solid things, but planks and boards get a tone each.
4. Clean, chunky shapes over fine detail; small parts bigger so they read; detail only where it carries meaning.
5. Fix glitches (jagged or broken faces).
6. Dark for holes and depth.
