	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Tiny_Treats_Pleasant_Picnic_1.0_FREE/`.
	The pack is one .gltf per model with two shared textures. 32 of the 41
	models are loaded; the cooler, radio, thermos, drink can, frisbee and the
	green blanket and pillow set are left in the missing tab.


	Scale

	One scale factor for the whole pack (style guide §6, P10): 0,24, the factor
	the other Tiny Treats kits at this size use. It is set by comparison:
	`plate` lands at 0,03 high beside isa-kitchen/plate at 0,03, `apple` at
	0,09 beside isa-food/bread at 0,12. At the 0,32 of isa-kitchen the large
	blanket would run 1,60 long against the 1,20 limit on obj-furniture.


	Names

	The pack names in mixed case with underscores; here that is kebab-case with
	a name that says what the thing is. A recoloured model is named after the
	band it landed on, as the catalogue does elsewhere. The source name is in
	every .glb under `asset.extras.taaleiland.bronmodel`.

  apple / apple_cut / apple_piece      -> apple, apple-cut, apple-piece
  cheese_A / cheese_B                  -> cheese-wedge, cheese-wheel
  picnic_basket_round / _square        -> basket-round, basket-square
  picnic_blanket_red / _blue           -> picnic-blanket-sienna, -hunter
  picnic_blanket_*_folded              -> picnic-blanket-*-folded
  pillow_large_red / _blue             -> pillow-large-sienna, -hunter
  pillow_small_red / _blue             -> pillow-small-sienna, -hunter
  plate_A / plate_B                    -> plate, plate-flower
  serving_tray_round / _square_A / _B  -> tray-round, tray-square,
                                          tray-square-handles
  jam                                  -> jam-jar


	Colour

	The pack has its own colour sheet: an 8 x 8 grid, each column holding four
	colours and each colour a base row and a shadow row under it. That sheet is
	not carried over — every source cell is mapped to a cell of the shared
	kits/colormap.png and each triangle keeps its place inside the cell, so the
	baked light-to-dark reading survives. A base row and its shadow row are one
	source colour and land on one band.

  white, cream          -> ceramic, ivory        light wood  -> wood-worked, camel
  pink, light blue      -> ceramic, terracotta   dark wood   -> wood-beam, chestnut
  grey                  -> metal-iron-steel      brown stem  -> vegetation, umber
  yellow                -> food, amber           green       -> food/vegetation, moss
  orange-red            -> food, sienna          dough       -> food, tan

	The blankets, the large pillows and the lid cloth on the jam jar carry a
	separate gingham texture instead of the colour sheet. Their triangles are
	read from that texture and mapped per colour: white to textile ivory, red
	to textile sienna, blue to textile hunter. The check survives where a
	triangle falls on a white square.

	Blue has no textile band — `textile` draws from ivory, hunter, taupe and
	sienna only (§5.1) — so the blue blanket and pillows read as hunter and are
	named for it. The teal mug and teapot land on ceramic ivory and terracotta
	for the same reason, and the light-blue plate rim on terracotta, which is
	what obj-kitchenware-tableware-plate allows a ceramic to take (§5.2).

	`cheese-wheel` is white in the source and amber here: B33 gives cheese
	`amber` whatever the source did.

	The green bottle and the goblet are `glass`, which is not band-checked
	(§5.1), and sit on hunter.


	What happened to the files is in tools/importeer/tiny-treats.mjs: scaled,
	origin at y=0 with the footprint centred, vertices re-welded, and written
	as one draw call. The scale sits on the root node, not in the vertices.
