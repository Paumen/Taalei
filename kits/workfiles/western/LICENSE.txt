	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Low_Poly_Western_Objects/`: one .fbx with
	every object as its own mesh, split per mesh, and a second zip with
	the texture map. Neither names an author or pack page.

	18 of the 48 objects are loaded. The well's shaft, which ran nine times
	its visible height below the rim, is cut off at ground level.


	Scale

	0.002. The bed lands at 1.08, the chair-height objects near 0.4.
	Fences come out at 0.19 high and carry scale-small; the dead tree at
	0.70 also carries scale-small.


	Colour

	Texture colours, averaged per flat face, are mapped to bands of
	kits/colormap.png by tools/import/adopt.mjs: wood to chestnut and
	camel, iron to basalt (slate on the water tower), stone to taupe,
	cactus to moss, lantern and bulb glass to amber.
