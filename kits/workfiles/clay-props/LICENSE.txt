	Origin and edits

	Names written together are split and lower-cased: CrockpotLid
	becomes crockpot-lid and WideVase becomes wide-vase. No model was
	skipped: all 10 models of the free tier are in the kit.

	Scaled to 0,65, against the tableware already in the catalogue.
	plate measures 0,20 wide, exactly restaurant/plate and
	dungeon/plate, and bowl 0,19 beside restaurant/bowl at 0,2. jug
	comes to 0,22 × 0,23 beside props/jug-a at 0,2 × 0,2.

	What happened to the files is in tools/importeer/aanvullen.mjs:
	converted to .glb, origin at y = 0 and the footprint centred in X
	and Z. The scale (0,65) is on the root node, not in the vertices.

	This pack does not use a grid of bands but a painted albedo
	texture, plus a normal and a roughness map. Those last two did not
	come along — the style guide asks for roughness 1 and metallic 0
	(§1). The albedo is sampled per triangle and mapped onto the shared
	kits/colormap.png, so the models share their colours with the other
	kits and take part in the same colour filter.

	Compared in Oklab, where equal distance means roughly equal visible
	difference; the distances below are in that measure, on a scale
	where 100 is the whole range.

	The whole pack is one material, fired clay, and so lands on one
	band: terracotta 5,0. The largest jump is the crockpot and the
	wide-vase at 7,7 — the pack's burnt red-brown (#9f4624) is a little
	lighter and more orange on the shared sheet.

	Two things to know. The models are smooth-shaded around a round
	cross-section rather than flat-faceted; they therefore carry
	`ngons`, and §2 counts their faces. And because each model uses
	exactly one band, one material is tagged per model, `ceramic`,
	which meets N1 to N3 exactly.

	Every vertex was split from the triangles it shares before
	remapping, otherwise one vertex would drag the colours of two
	neighbouring faces over each other. Equal vertices (same position,
	normal and UV) were then merged again. The geometry is untouched:
	no triangle was added, removed or moved.
