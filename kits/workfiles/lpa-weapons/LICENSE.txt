	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Low_Poly_Medieval_Weapons/`, one zip of
	.fbx files in category folders. It holds no license file and names no
	author or pack page. Its layout and flat materials match the other
	`lpa` packs (lpa-tools, lpa-desert), so it carries the same artist tag.

	47 of the 62 models are loaded. Seige Tower and Falchion Cleaver are
	identical copies of Siege Tower and Falchion and are rejected as
	duplicates.


	Scale

	0.00354, the lpa-tools factor: the pack shares its units. The sword
	lands at 0.58 and the long bow at 0.83, inside the catalogue's sword
	and bow ranges.


	Colour

	Flat materials are mapped to bands of kits/colormap.png by
	tools/import/adopt.mjs: steel to nickel, handles to camel or chestnut,
	grips and straps to umber, siege wood to chestnut, camel and tan with
	cast-iron fittings on slate, stone ammunition to taupe.
