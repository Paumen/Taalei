	Origin

	The source files come as .obj with one .mtl per model, in a zip named
	`modular_house_collection`, without a licence file, maker or pack page.
	The CC0 label is the project owner's; it does not come from the pack.

	The workfiles are converted with tools/import/adopt.mjs: the pack's flat
	material colours are mapped onto the shared kits/colormap.png, the
	geometry is scaled by 0.45 on the root node, grounded and centred.
