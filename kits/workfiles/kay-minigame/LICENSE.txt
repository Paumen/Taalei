	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/KayKit_Mini-Game_Variety_Pack_1.2/`.
	The pack is one .glb per model. 3 of the 132 models are loaded: the bomb
	and the two plants. The rest is team-coloured game furniture — gates,
	flags, hoops, blasters, barriers — that has no place in the catalogue and
	stays in the missing tab.


	Scale

	One scale factor for the whole pack (style guide §6, P10): 0,30, the
	standard factor for KayKit packs. It is set by comparison: `pine` lands at
	0,90 high beside kay-forest/tree-1-a at 1,25 and isa-park/tree at 0,87.


	Names

	The pack names in mixed case; here that is kebab-case with a name that says
	what the thing is. The source name is in every .glb under
	`asset.extras.taaleiland.bronmodel`.

  powerupBomb  -> bomb
  tree_desert  -> cactus
  tree_forest  -> pine

	`tree_desert` is a saguaro and resolves to `env-flora-plant-cactus` by
	shape; the kit of origin does not decide the kind (§3, T02).


	Colour

	The source paints one flat colour per material, four in all. None of them
	came along: each is mapped to a band of the shared kits/colormap.png and
	placed in that band at the height whose luminance matches the source
	colour, shaded along normal Y, so the relative light-to-dark reading is
	kept.

  GreenDark  -> foliage, hunter / vegetation, hunter
  BrownDark  -> wood-bark, umber
  BlackDark  -> metal-iron-cast, slate
  Beige      -> rope, taupe

	The bands follow §4 and §5 of the style guide.


	What happened to the files is in tools/importeer/flat-packs.mjs: scaled,
	origin at y=0 with the footprint centred, vertices re-welded, normals
	smoothed at 60°, and written as one draw call.
