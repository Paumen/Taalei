	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Scythian_Decoration/`, two zips of the same
	model: one .glb, one .obj with .fbx and .mtl. Neither holds a license file
	and neither names an author or pack page. The CC0 note above was set by the
	owner of this project on import; it does not come from the pack itself. The
	author is unknown, so there is nobody to credit — whoever traces the origin
	adds it here.

	The .obj is the one read. The pack is one model and it is loaded.


	Scale

	One scale factor for the whole pack (style guide §6, P10): 1, so the source
	units are metres. It is set by comparison: the plaque lands at 0,25 × 0,25
	beside asia-pack/wall-decor-a at 0,25 × 0,20 and under asia-rg/dragon-
	ornament at 0,85 long.


	Names

	ScythianAntique -> ornament


	Colour

	The source paints one flat colour, #e4a95d, over the whole mesh. It does not
	come along: the model is mapped to metal-gold on the amber band of the
	shared kits/colormap.png, placed in the band at the height whose luminance
	matches the source colour and shaded along normal Y, so the baked light-to-
	dark reading is kept.


	Where it sits outside the style guide

	The plaque is Scythian animal-style openwork: interlaced beasts cut out of a
	sheet 7 mm thick. That is thin and busy where §1 asks for chunky and few
	details (I01, I03), and its strands are narrower than the 15 mm of G02 — the
	measured minimum edge is 0. At 4088 triangles over a 0,25 × 0,25 × 0,05
	footprint the tri budget comes to 11918 against the 10000 ceiling on obj
	(G04), which the size lint carries as a warning. It is in the catalogue at
	the owner's request.


	What happened to the file is in tools/importeer/ontbrekend.mjs: scaled,
	origin at y=0 with the footprint centred, vertices re-welded, normals
	smoothed at 60°, and written as one draw call. The scale sits on the root
	node, not in the vertices.
