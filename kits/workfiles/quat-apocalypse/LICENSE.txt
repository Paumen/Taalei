	Origin

	The source is `kits/sources/Post_Apocolypse_Pack/`. It holds no license
	file and names no author or pack page. The CC0 note above was set by the
	owner of this project on import; it does not come from the pack itself.
	The author is unknown, so there is nobody to credit — whoever traces the
	origin adds it here.

	The pack is one .glb per model with two shared texture atlases. 19 of the
	54 models are loaded: the four survivors, the two dogs, the hand weapons
	and the street props. The vehicles, the road and building pieces, the
	zombie parts and the blood decals are left in the missing tab.


	Scale

	One scale factor for the whole pack (style guide §6, P10): 0,45. It is set
	by comparison with the catalogue's other characters: `survivor-matt` lands
	at 0,68 high beside kay-adventurers/knight at 0,76 and
	kay-skeleton/skeleton-warrior at 0,78.

	`dog-pug` lands at 0,35 high against the 0,40 minimum on char — a warning,
	not an error. The one pack factor wins (§6, P10).


	Names

	The pack names in mixed case and hangs a download hash on repeats; here
	that is kebab-case with a name that says what the thing is. A recoloured
	model is named after the band it landed on. The source name is in every
	.glb under `asset.extras.taaleiland.bronmodel`.

  Characters Matt / Sam / Shaun  -> survivor-matt, survivor-sam,
                                    survivor-shaun
  Lis                            -> survivor-lis
  Characters Pug                 -> dog-pug
  German Shepard                 -> dog-shepherd
  Chest / Chest-RfSBvgcZUD       -> chest-hunter, chest-sienna
  Wooden Bat Barbed              -> bat-barbed
  Pallet / Pallet Broken         -> pallet, pallet-broken
  Trash Bag / Trash Bags         -> trash-bag, trash-bags


	Colour

	The source paints from two texture atlases of flat swatches, so every
	triangle reads one colour. None of them came along: each is mapped to a
	band of the shared kits/colormap.png and placed in that band at the height
	whose luminance matches the source colour, shaded along normal Y, so the
	relative light-to-dark reading is kept.

  light metal    -> metal-iron-steel, nickel   dark metal -> metal-iron-cast
  pale wood      -> wood-planks, tan           gun stock  -> wood-beam
  tool handle    -> wood-worked, camel         grip wrap  -> rope, taupe
  skin, fur      -> skin (tan, umber, taupe)   dark cloth -> leather, umber
  orange, red    -> textile, sienna            green      -> textile, hunter
  green paint    -> metal, hunter              red paint  -> metal, sienna

	The wrapped grips on `axe`, `spear` and the survivors take `rope` on taupe,
	which is what B40 asks of a wrapped grip. The painted chests, the hydrant
	and the pipes stay red, green and yellow on the parent `metal`, which
	carries no palette of its own; the chests carry `metal-iron-cast` on their
	fittings so the `mat.metal` rule on obj-container-chest holds, and
	`wood-beam` on their panels for the `has` rule.

	A character is held to six bands (G14) and to ivory, hunter and sienna for
	textile (§5.2), so the survivors' off-whites, blues and blacks are folded
	into leather, hunter and the metal they already carry. Hair has no material
	of its own and reads as `skin`.

	The black bin bags take `textile` on taupe, the darkest band that palette
	allows.


	What happened to the files is in tools/importeer/flat-packs.mjs: scaled,
	origin at y=0 with the footprint centred, vertices re-welded, normals
	smoothed at 60°, and written as one draw call. The source rigs and
	animations are not carried over.
